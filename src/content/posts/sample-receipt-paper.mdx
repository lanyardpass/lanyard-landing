When you share your season from Lanyard, it doesn't export a chart. It prints a receipt, the long thermal kind, with your visits as line items and a rubber stamp when the pass pays for itself.

Skeuomorphism has been out of fashion for a decade, and mostly for good reason. But a receipt is the one object everyone already knows how to read as proof of money. The moment your season takes that shape, the question "was the pass worth it" stops being abstract. There it is, itemized, with a total at the bottom, the same way every other purchase in your life justifies itself.

The stamp earns its place the same way. A green PAID IN FULL slammed over the total says something a progress bar never will, because a progress bar is a promise and a stamp is a verdict.

Every number on it comes from the same math the app uses everywhere else. The paper is theater. The total is not.
