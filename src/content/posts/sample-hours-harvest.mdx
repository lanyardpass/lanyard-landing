Park hours look like the most boring data we carry. They're also the data most likely to ruin someone's evening, because an hour shaved off closing time is a fireworks show you drove forty minutes to miss.

So the hours never come from a spreadsheet we typed once and hoped about. Every night a harvest job asks each operator's own calendar what tomorrow holds, and the answer gets compared against what we already believed. When the two agree, nothing happens, which is the good kind of nothing. When they disagree, a human looks before anything ships.

The interesting cases are the quiet ones. A park that closes at six on a Tuesday in September isn't news to the operator, but it's absolutely news to a passholder who assumed nine. Those early closes are exactly the thing the app exists to catch, and the harvest is where they enter the building.

The rule underneath it all is simple enough to print on a shirt. Data you were sure about last month is data worth checking tonight.
